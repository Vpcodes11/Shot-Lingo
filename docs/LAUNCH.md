## Direct outreach (first 10 paying users)

Find small apps on the App Store/Play with plain or English-only screenshots in non-English markets. Message the developer (X, email from the listing):

> Hi {name}, I like {app}. I noticed your screenshots are English-only in the {country} store. I made ShotLingo, which translates screenshot captions and listing text and exports every size. I made a {language} version of your first screenshot for free: {image}. Happy to give you Pro free for a month if you want to try it on the full set.

Do 10 a day. Offering a finished sample image is what gets replies.
